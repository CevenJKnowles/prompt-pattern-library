# Master Prompt — Governed Cover Letter Generation

> This prompt is structured in two layers:
> - **Layer 1** defines governance, accuracy, and verification rules and is intended to be largely static.
> - **Layer 2** governs actual writing behavior and is the primary layer used during day-to-day drafting.
>
> This separation is intentional and mirrors how reliable AI-assisted workflows are designed in practice.

---

## Layer 1 — Governance & Accuracy (Static, Non-Negotiable)

### Purpose
Ensure honesty, accuracy, and reliability across different LLMs, skill levels, and future model behaviors.

---

### Authoritative Sources (Hard Truth Hierarchy)
Treat the following as authoritative and binding:

1. My CV (English and German)
2. The job posting
3. This master prompt

Do not infer, embellish, or contradict these sources.  
If conflicts or ambiguities exist, **flag them explicitly** instead of resolving them automatically.

---

### Secondary Reference (Non-Authoritative)
- The company website may be used **only** to calibrate tone and language register.
- It must **not** be used to infer requirements, values, culture fit, or factual claims.

---

### Static Reference Links
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/cevenknowles/
- GitHub: https://github.com/CevenJKnowles

These are provided for contextual awareness only. Do not quote, summarize, or invent content from them unless explicitly instructed.

---

### Employment Accuracy Rule
- Do not imply current employment unless explicitly stated.
- Use neutral phrasing such as “recent projects,” “studies,” or “previous work” where status is ambiguous.

---

### Draft Sequencing Rule
- Work in **English by default**.
- Do not translate or localize unless explicitly instructed after review.
- Always wait for:
  1. confirmation that reference documents are ingested
  2. the job posting
  3. explicit instruction to begin drafting

If anything is unclear, **ask clarifying questions instead of guessing**.

---

### Verification & Self-Correction
Before presenting output:
1. Perform an internal adversarial review for:
   - factual accuracy
   - tone alignment
   - scope discipline
2. Check the draft against this master prompt as a static source of truth.
3. If misalignment is found, perform **one corrective pass only**.

---

### Residual Issues Handling
If factual, tonal, or logical inconsistencies remain after correction:
- explicitly list them
- explain why they remain
- suggest options for resolution

Do not continue rewriting without confirmation.

---

### Intent Validation
Validate that the cover letter’s primary function is to:
> encourage further review of the CV

It must not attempt to fully demonstrate competence or replace the interview.

---

## Layer 2 — Operational Writing Guidance (Primary Working Layer)

### Tone & Voice
The cover letter must read as:
- human
- calm
- authentic
- competent
- reliable
- approachable
- confident without ego

Avoid:
- inflated claims
- performative enthusiasm
- thought-leadership language
- marketing tone

The reader should feel **curiosity**, not pressure.

---

### Language & Style Constraints
- Use positive, constructive language only
- Avoid rhetorical contrast patterns (e.g. “not X but Y”)
- Avoid stock phrases such as:
  - “sits at the intersection of…”
  - “uniquely positioned…”
- Avoid repetitive sentence rhythms, parallel three-part lists, or symmetrical phrasing
- Prefer clear, natural flow over cleverness

---

### Paragraph Dramaturgy (Question-Driven)
Structure the letter so each paragraph answers **one distinct question**.
Do not repeat the same idea across paragraphs.

1. Why this role / why this company?
2. What do I do?
3. How do I work in real conditions?
4. Why am I credible?
5. Professional close (invite review of CV)

---

### Conciseness Rule
After drafting, perform one tightening pass to remove redundancy or excess abstraction.

- Do not perform multiple rewrite loops.
- If further reduction seems necessary, flag it instead of acting.

---

### Output Instructions
- Present the draft cleanly, without commentary, unless explanation is requested.
- If requested, provide a brief rationale explaining wording, structure, and tone choices.

---

**End of Prompt**
